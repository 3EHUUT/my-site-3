How to Choose the Right Fitness Gear

Five principles we follow when testing every product

1

Match gear to your actual routine

A trail runner and a yoga practitioner need completely different investments. Before spending, write down your three most frequent weekly activities and prioritize gear for those first.

2

Don't overlook durability per dollar

A $30 water bottle that lasts five years beats a $12 one you replace annually. Check materials — stainless steel, vulcanized rubber, and ripstop nylon are signs a product is built to survive.

3

Test wearables for comfort, not just features

The most accurate fitness tracker is useless if it chafes your wrist or dies mid-run. Prioritize battery life and band comfort over having seventeen sport modes you'll never use.

4

Read the one-star reviews

Five-star reviews tell you what's great. One-star reviews tell you what breaks. Look for patterns — if multiple people mention the same failure point, take it seriously.

5

Consider the ecosystem

Some brands lock you into proprietary apps, chargers, or accessories. If you already own Garmin watches, a Garmin cycling computer will sync seamlessly. Mixing ecosystems creates friction.

Most orders ship within 24 hours. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across the continental US. Expedited 2-day shipping is available at checkout for a flat $9.99.

We accept returns within 30 days, even if you've used the product. We just ask that it's clean and in its original packaging. Wearables must be factory-reset before return.

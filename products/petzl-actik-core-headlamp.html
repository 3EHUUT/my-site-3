About This Product

Petzl's Actik Core is the headlamp for serious outdoor adventurers who need reliability. 600 lumens with a 160-meter beam illuminates trails far ahead. The rechargeable CORE battery with AAA backup means you're never stranded in the dark. Red lighting preserves night vision. 130 hours on low keeps it going for week-long treks.

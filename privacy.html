Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

Iron Trail ("we," "us," or "our") operates an affiliate e-commerce website focused on outdoor gear, apparel, and accessories. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site.

Information We Collect

Usage Data

When you browse our site, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation paths
  • Referring website or source that led you to our site
  • Date and time of each visit

Device Information

We may collect information about the device you use to access our site, including:

  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile)
  • Operating system and version
  • Browser type and version
  • Screen resolution and language preference
  • IP address (anonymized where required by law)

Newsletter Subscription

We offer an optional newsletter featuring outdoor gear recommendations, product reviews, and exclusive deals. If you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address. Newsletter signup is entirely voluntary and requires your explicit consent. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in every email.

How We Use Cookies

Iron Trail uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience. We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the website to function properly, including remembering your cookie consent preferences and enabling basic site navigation.
  • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with our site, which pages are most popular, and how we can improve our content. We use this data in aggregate form only.
  • Affiliate cookies: When you click on a product link, our affiliate partners may place cookies on your device to track referrals. These cookies allow us to earn a commission if you make a purchase through our links, at no additional cost to you.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain our website
  • Analyze site traffic and improve our content
  • Send newsletter emails to subscribers who have opted in
  • Ensure proper attribution of affiliate referrals
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or abuse

We Do Not Sell or Trade Your Data

Iron Trail does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share your email address or any personally identifiable information with outside parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our site may contain links to third-party retailer websites. When you click an affiliate link, you leave our site and are subject to that retailer's privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of any third-party site you visit. We work with affiliate networks and analytics providers who may process data on our behalf under strict contractual obligations.

Data Retention

We retain usage and analytics data in anonymized form for up to 26 months. Newsletter subscriber email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion. Upon unsubscribing, your email address is removed from our active mailing list within 30 days.

Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your personal data for certain purposes.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on your consent.

Your Rights Under CCPA

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information — though Iron Trail does not sell personal data.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please reach out to us through the contact page on our website. We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.
